The bundled fusion-pixel-12px-monospaced-zh_hans.otf is a subset of Fusion Pixel Font 2026.09.25
(https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font): only ASCII / Latin-1, punctuation and symbols, the GB2312
characters and the characters used by Folmi's interface are kept, and hinting is dropped. No glyph was
changed. It is distributed under the same SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt). The original file is kept in the
source repository at third_party/fonts/; scripts/subset-pixel-font.py makes the subset.
